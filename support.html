Apple Health and pulse

How do I connect Apple Health?

In Jarl, go to You, then Heart rate, then Connect, and tap Allow. On Apple’s screen, tap Turn On All, then Allow. You can change this later in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Jarl.

Why does my pulse show “Waiting for watch”?

An Apple Watch sends heart rate to your iPhone every few minutes, unless a workout is running on the watch. For live pulse, start a Traditional Strength Training workout on your Apple Watch when you start a training in Jarl, and end it when you finish.

Can I use a Garmin watch?

Yes. In the Garmin Connect app, turn on syncing with Apple Health. Jarl then reads your resting pulse and other values. Pulse during training arrives when the watch syncs, so it isn’t live.

Why is my VO₂ max empty?

Apple Watch estimates VO₂ max during outdoor walks, runs or hikes of at least 20 minutes. It appears in Jarl once Apple Health has a value.

I take medication that lowers my heart rate

Go to You, then Heart rate, and turn on I take medication that lowers my heart rate. Jarl then guides you by breathing and effort instead of pulse zones.

Training

How do I change my training days?

Go to You, then Training days, and pick 2 to 6 days. To move a single training this week, tap the day in the week strip on the Today tab.

What is a light day?

When you train two days in a row, the second training becomes lighter: two sets per exercise at the same weights. It helps you recover, which matters more after 50.

An exercise hurts. What should I do?

Stop the exercise. Tap Swap exercise for a joint-friendly version, or tell Jarl which joint is sore in the check-in before your training. If pain lasts more than a few days, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Jarl has no accounts or servers. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In Jarl, go to You and tap Reset the app twice. Or delete the app from your iPhone.

I got a new iPhone. Where is my history?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Jarl’s data is included in those backups.

Subscription

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Jarl, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Jarl is a training guide, not medical advice. If you have heart problems, high blood pressure or recent surgery, check with your doctor before you start. In an emergency, call 113 in Norway or your local emergency number.
